01 / What we build

Four kinds of system. All of them yours.

01

Custom AI applications

Product-grade systems with a model at the core — retrieval, evals, guardrails, UI. Not a chatbot bolted onto a CRUD app.

02

Your tools already hold the data. We make them act on it — API-level integrations, not brittle no-code chains someone has to babysit.
